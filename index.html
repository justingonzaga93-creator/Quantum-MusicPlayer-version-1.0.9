About & Legalv1.0.9
Privacy Policy

Quantum Music Player processes selected audio files locally in this browser. Imported audio and library records are saved in this browser's site storage when available. This app does not intentionally upload your music to a developer server. Browser storage can be cleared by your device or browser, so keep original audio files and use backup controls where available.

App Permissions

The app requests access only to files you choose through the system file picker. Audio playback and visualizer features use browser media and audio APIs when supported.

Data & Storage

Your library is tied to this browser and app origin. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or opening a different app address may make saved items unavailable. Playlist entries refer to songs saved in this app's library.

Security & Backup

Keep a copy of your original audio files. Export a library backup before major updates when backup is available. A library backup may contain song records and artwork but may not include every original audio file depending on browser limits.

App Updates

Quantum Music Player v1.0.9. Keep the same app origin when updating to help preserve browser-stored data.
